Our Story

A kitchen built on Caribbean roots, conscious living, and twenty years of love for the craft.

The Beginning

Where Island Flavours
Found a Nova Scotia Home

Café Caye Mangé was born from a simple but powerful idea: that food rooted in Caribbean culture could be fully plant-based, deeply nourishing, and absolutely delicious.

Chef Pamela Alexander-Leonforde brings over 20 years of culinary experience to every plate, drawing inspiration from the vibrant food traditions of the Caribbean islands: the bold spices, tropical ingredients, and the warmth that makes every meal feel like a celebration.

What began as a passion became a purpose: to offer Yarmouth and beyond a dining experience that honours both the planet and the palate. Every dish at Café Caye Mangé is a testament to the belief that vegan food isn't a limitation, but an invitation to discover flavours you never knew existed.

Today, with a growing community of plant-based food lovers across Nova Scotia and plenty of familiar faces returning week after week, the café continues to grow, rooted in the same values it started with.

Our Values
