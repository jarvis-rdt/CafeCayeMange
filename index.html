Where the Islands
Meet Your Table

Born of a deep love for Caribbean culture and a commitment to conscious living, Café Caye Mangé brings the warmth of the islands to the heart of Yarmouth, Nova Scotia.

Every dish is thoughtfully crafted using organic, fresh produce sourced from certified farms — because real flavour starts with real ingredients.

Chef Pamela A. Leonforde
